Your PC, always on display

AODexa turns your idle Windows screen into a clean always-on display with clocks, calendars and more than 100 widgets. It works fully offline.

Turn it on any time with ScrollLock or Ctrl + Shift + G.

What you get

Clocks and calendarsAnalog and digital clocks, plus calendar widgets, that update in real time.
100+ widgetsPreview layouts and themes in the Widget Gallery and apply one instantly.
Auto-idleThe display starts after the idle time you choose and closes on any input.
Fully offlineNo internet connection is needed. Everything runs on your PC.
Two shortcutsToggle the display with ScrollLock or Ctrl + Shift + G.

What’s new

Fully offlineWeather and prayer times have been removed, so AODexa no longer uses an internet connection.
New shortcutCtrl + Shift + G now works alongside ScrollLock.
More widgetsOver 100 clock and calendar widgets to choose from.
