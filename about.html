Meet AODexa

Transform your Windows PC into a beautiful always-on display. Glanceable info, elegant widgets, zero distraction.

Current Version 1.0.0  ·  Windows 10 / 11
What is AODexa

AODexa is a Windows desktop application that turns your idle screen into a stunning always-on display — just like the lock screens on modern smartphones. When your keyboard and mouse are inactive, AODexa automatically activates a full-screen display showing the time, date, weather, prayer times, inspirational quotes, and more. Press ScrollLock anytime to toggle it instantly.

Features

Live Clock & Date

Beautiful analog and digital clock with real-time updates.

Weather Widget

Current weather with location detection, updated automatically.

Prayer Times

Accurate daily prayer schedules based on your location.

Quotes & Messages

Rotating inspirational quotes and custom messages.

Widget Gallery

Pick from multiple layouts and themes in the gallery.

Auto-Idle Detection

Activates after your chosen idle time. Dismisses on any input.
