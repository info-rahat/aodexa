Before you contact us

Check the Help page

Most common questions are answered in the Help Center.

AOD won’t start automatically

Make sure the AOD Master Switch and Auto-Idle Mode are both on in Settings, and that AODexa is running in the system tray.

AOD won’t open on demand

Try both shortcuts: ScrollLock and Ctrl + Shift + G.
