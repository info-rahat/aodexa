Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

AODexa works fully offline. It does not collect, store or share personal data, and all settings stay on your device.

1. What the app does on your device

Idle detectionAODexa checks locally whether your keyboard and mouse are idle or active to start the display. It never reads, records or sends what you type or click.
App settingsYour widget choice, idle time and other preferences are saved in a file on your device.

2. What we do not collect

Your name, email or any account information.
Keystrokes or mouse content. Only the idle or active state is used.
Crash reports or analytics.
Advertising or tracking data. AODexa has no ad or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share data.

3. Internet and location

AODexa no longer fetches weather or prayer times, and it does not use your location or any third-party service. We do not operate servers, databases or user accounts.

4. Data storage and deletion

All data stays on your Windows device. To delete it, uninstall AODexa, or go to Windows Settings → Apps → AODexa → Advanced options → Reset.

5. Children’s privacy

AODexa does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app has no account system and collects no data.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date.
